CARNARVON BALL CLUB - ONE-PAGE BUYER'S BRIEF
Greater Victoria, BC | Checked 7 Aug 2026 | CAD

VERDICT
Good to very good value for Oak Bay/east Saanich families. Best current bargain:
spring girls' basketball at $100 for ten sessions (~$10/session). Fastball is
competitively priced. Get a written baseball quote because current spring fees are
not publicly displayed.

WHAT IT OFFERS
- Baseball: co-ed 5U-18U plus 19+ pathway; spring, fall, clinics and competitive
  summer/all-star options.
- Fastball: U7-U15; spring, fall and winter development.
- Girls' basketball: Grades 3-9; recreational Night League, Club Skills and
  competitive Selects/travel teams. Beginners welcome.

2026 PRICE SNAPSHOT
Fastball: U7 $135 | U9 $185 | U11/U13/U15 $325 total.
Spring basketball: $100 for ten Monday sessions (Mar 30-Jun 22); own ball/shoes.
Baseball: current fee not posted. Local benchmark: National Little League $90-$300;
Gordon Head Baseball $130-$395 plus refundable $200 volunteer deposit.
Allow for equipment, travel/tournaments, higher-level team fees and volunteer time.

WHERE
Six Oak Bay/Saanich parks: Carnarvon, Fireman's (fastball hub), Henderson, Windsor,
Allenby and Maynard. Basketball uses local school gyms; spring skills are at
St. Margaret's School, 1080 Lucas Ave. Night League venues/schedules vary.

WHY IT CAN BE WORTH IT
- Broad recreational-to-competitive pathway in one community non-profit.
- Large girls-only basketball environment; skills included with Night League.
- Strong convenience for nearby residents and credible inter-club competition.

WATCH-OUTS
Volunteer-run delivery; weekday schedules can vary; some programs fill quickly;
travel and competitive teams cost extra; catchment may matter. Refund deadlines are
strict. Confirm inclusions, volunteer deposit/hours, equipment and weekly schedule.

NEARBY ALTERNATIVES
Baseball: Gordon Head, National, Layritz, Beacon Hill.
Fastball: Cordova Bay and Beacon Hill.
Basketball: other Victoria Night League clubs; Bay Nation is a nearby boys' option.

BOTTOM LINE
Choose Carnarvon when location and team culture fit. Basketball skills are excellent
value; fastball is fair-to-good value. For baseball, compare a current Carnarvon quote
with Gordon Head/National before paying.
