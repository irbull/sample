On a rainy evening in a tiny harbor town, Mira found a brass key tucked inside an old library book.

The key had no label, only a star etched on one side. Curious, she wandered the cobblestone streets until she noticed the same star on a weathered blue door behind the clocktower.

Inside was a narrow room filled with maps, journals, and a lantern that lit itself when she stepped in. On the desk sat a note:

"If you found the key, the next chapter is yours to write."

Mira smiled, opened a blank journal, and began.
