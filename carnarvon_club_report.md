# Carnarvon Ball Club — Research Report

**Greater Victoria, British Columbia | Research checked 7 August 2026 | Prices in CAD**

## Executive assessment

Carnarvon Ball Club is a volunteer-run, community non-profit offering baseball, fast-pitch softball (“fastball”), and girls’ basketball. It is a particularly strong fit for families in Oak Bay and nearby Saanich who want a broad local pathway: beginner-to-competitive baseball and fastball, plus a girls-centred basketball program that spans recreational Night League, skills training and travel teams.

**Value verdict: good to very good, provided the locations and volunteer expectations suit your family.** The clearest bargain is the current spring basketball skills program: **$100 for ten sessions (about $10 per session)**. The published 2026 fastball total of **$135–$325** is broadly reasonable for a season of organized youth sport and includes the Softball BC component. Baseball pricing is not presently published on the club page, so it should be confirmed before paying; nearby 2026 baseball programs provide a useful benchmark of roughly **$90–$395**, depending on age and organization.

## What the club offers

### Baseball

- Co-ed programming from introductory 5U through 18U, plus a 19+ South Island Baseball League pathway.
- Regular spring season, fall ball and off-season development clinics.
- More competitive opportunities include Prospects/all-star teams and provincial/national competition; some carry extra fees.
- At 11U and older, teams interlock with other Greater Victoria parks. Published division pages describe assessments, volunteer coaches and supplied team apparel in some divisions.
- The live registration page currently advertises fall ball and later-season opportunities rather than 2026 spring fees. **Confirm the exact fee, inclusions, refund terms, equipment and any volunteer deposit before registering.**

### Fastball / fast-pitch softball

- Youth divisions currently listed from U7 through U15, with B/C-level team options at older ages.
- Spring season, fall program and winter clinics; the club’s main field is Fireman’s Park.
- **Published 2026 spring totals:** U7 $135; U9 $185; U11B/U11C $325; U13B/U13C $325; U15C $325. These totals combine a $35–$45 Softball BC/RAMP fee with the $100–$290 club fee.
- Registration is a two-step process: Softball BC/RAMP first, then the Carnarvon payment link.
- Softball BC also lists a **$10 one-time lifetime member number**; ask whether that applies if the player is new, as it is not shown in Carnarvon’s total table.

### Girls’ basketball

- Girls-focused programming for Grades 3–9 (with trans, non-binary and gender-diverse participation considered case by case).
- Fall/winter Victoria Night League: one weekday practice and one weekday game each week, September–February. U11 placement emphasizes friend groups; U13/U15 uses assessments to create appropriately matched teams.
- Competitive Carnarvon Selects for Grades 6–8 add practices and mainland tournaments; travel and additional program costs should be budgeted separately.
- Club Skills is included with fall/winter Night League registration, or was available separately for $50 in the published 2025/26 information.
- **Current 2026 spring skills:** $100 for ten Monday sessions, 30 March–22 June, at St. Margaret’s School. U11 is 6–7 p.m.; U13/U15 is 7–8 p.m. Players bring their own basketball and shoes.
- Spring skills refund terms: full refund less $25 before the program starts; 50% before 30 April 2026. The named dates have passed as of this report, so confirm availability and current refund eligibility.

## Where it operates

Carnarvon does not operate from one clubhouse. Baseball and fastball use six parks across Oak Bay and Saanich:

- **Carnarvon Park** — Henderson Road at Carnarvon Street, Oak Bay
- **Fireman’s Park** — 1703 Monterey Avenue, Oak Bay (fastball hub)
- **Henderson Park** — 2291 Cedar Hill Cross Road, Oak Bay
- **Windsor Park** — 2451 Windsor Road, Oak Bay
- **Allenby Park** — near Allenby Street and Dean Avenue, Saanich
- **Maynard Park** — Saanich

Basketball uses local schools rather than a single club gym. Spring 2026 skills are at **St. Margaret’s School, 1080 Lucas Avenue, Saanich**; Night League practices and games rotate among Greater Victoria school gyms. The variable weekday schedule is the main convenience risk for busy families.

## Is it good value for your money?

| Test | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Direct price | Spring basketball is excellent value at about $10/session. Fastball is competitive for organized youth sport, especially U7/U9. Baseball cannot be rated precisely without a current quote. |
| Program depth | Strong: recreational and competitive routes, multiple seasons, skills clinics, and older-age pathways. |
| Coaching | Basketball advertises a technical director with 30+ years’ experience; team delivery still relies substantially on parent volunteers. Baseball/fastball are volunteer-driven. |
| Convenience | Excellent for Oak Bay/east Saanich residents; less attractive if school-gym travel, weeknight variability or cross-city interlock games create a long commute. |
| Hidden/extra cost risk | Moderate: personal glove/helmet/clothing, own basketball/shoes, tournament/travel fees, higher-level team supplements and volunteer time. Confirm deposits and inclusions. |

**Best value for:** a child who will attend regularly, wants a local team community, and may progress from recreational play into more competitive training. Carnarvon is especially compelling for girls who want a large girls-only basketball environment.

**Less compelling for:** families seeking a fixed weekend-only schedule, a fully professional coaching model, or the cheapest possible single-season baseball option. Catchment rules and available places may also determine the practical choice.

## Recommendation and pre-payment checklist

Shortlist Carnarvon if it is within a manageable drive and the child wants baseball/fastball development or a girls-centred basketball setting. The **$100 basketball skills program is an easy “yes” on price** if space remains. The **fastball fee is fair**, with the younger divisions particularly affordable. For baseball, compare Carnarvon’s written quote against Gordon Head and National before deciding.

Ask the registrar to confirm:

1. The exact fee for the player’s birth year/division and what is included.
2. Any volunteer deposit/hours, fundraising, uniform deposit or team levy.
3. Expected weekly practice/game schedule and travel radius.
4. Required personal equipment and estimated tournament/travel cost.
5. Catchment eligibility, waitlist status and the current refund deadline.
